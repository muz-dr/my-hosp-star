Quality indicators explained

Overall quality follows the KKM HPIA 2025 Weighted Percentile–Point–Scoring methodology.

HPIA
Hospital Performance Indicator Assessment
Patient safety, clinical effectiveness, and patient-centred care.
50% weight
Cluster
Cluster Hospital Performance
Collaboration and resource sharing within geographically linked hospital clusters.
25% weight
MyPSQ
Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire (PSQ-18)
Patient-reported experience: communication, cleanliness, wait times.
15% weight
Casemix
Casemix Performance
Case complexity and resource efficiency relative to severity.
10% weight
CliSQI
Clinical Services Quality Indicators
Specific clinical outcome metrics: infection rates, readmissions.
Informational

An information and comparison tool

Hospital performance indicators support informed comparison and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of individual clinical outcomes. Hospital choice may depend on clinical needs, specialist availability, referral requirements and other factors.

In an emergency, call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department immediately.

⚕️ This website is an information tool. It does not provide medical advice. Patient satisfaction reflects reported patient experience and should be considered alongside clinical and operational quality indicators. Data source: Ministry of Health Malaysia (Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia).
