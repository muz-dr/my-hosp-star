Quality indicators explained

The overall hospital quality score is calculated from five key indicator domains, following the KKM HPIA 2025 methodology.

HPIA
Hospital Performance Indicator Assessment
Measures hospital performance across patient safety, clinical effectiveness, and patient-centred care.
50% weight
Cluster
Cluster Hospital Performance
Evaluates how well hospitals within a geographically linked cluster collaborate and share resources.
25% weight
MyPSQ
Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire (PSQ-18)
Captures patient-reported experience covering communication, cleanliness, waiting times, and overall satisfaction.
15% weight
Casemix
Casemix Performance
Reflects the complexity of cases handled and the efficiency of resource use relative to case severity.
10% weight
CliSQI
Clinical Services Quality Indicators
Tracks specific clinical outcome metrics such as infection rates and unplanned readmissions.
Informational

An information and comparison tool

Hospital performance indicators are intended to support informed comparison and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of individual clinical outcomes. Hospital choice may depend on clinical needs, emergency status, specialist availability, referral requirements, and other factors.

In an emergency, call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department immediately.

⚕️ This website is an information tool. It does not provide medical advice and should not be used as the sole basis for choosing a hospital. Patient satisfaction reflects reported patient experience and should be considered alongside clinical and operational quality indicators.
