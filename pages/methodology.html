Overall Hospital Quality Score

The overall hospital quality score follows the KKM HPIA 2025 Weighted Percentile–Point–Scoring Concept (PPS-C). It combines four quality domains into a single composite score on a 0–5 scale.

Overall Score =
(HPIA Score × 0.50) +
(Cluster Score × 0.25) +
(MyPSQ Score × 0.15) +
(Casemix Score × 0.10)
DomainFull NameWeight
HPIAHospital Performance Indicator Assessment50%
ClusterCluster Hospital Performance25%
MyPSQPatient Satisfaction Questionnaire (PSQ-18)15%
CasemixCasemix Performance10%

If an authoritative overall score is present in the source dataset, that official score is displayed. The formula above is used only where no official composite score is provided.

The weights are configurable and will be updated as KKM publishes revised methodology for future reporting years.

HPIA 50% weight

Hospital Performance Indicator Assessment (HPIA) is the primary quality instrument used by KKM to evaluate hospital performance. It covers three major domains:

  • Patient Safety — measures adverse events, infection control, and medication safety
  • Clinical Effectiveness — measures outcomes, adherence to clinical standards, and evidence-based practice
  • Patient-Centred Care — measures communication, responsiveness, and respect for patient preferences

Scoring

HPIA uses a Percentile–Point–Scoring Concept (PPS-C). Each indicator is scored relative to all hospitals nationally, then weighted and aggregated into a composite HPIA score on a 1–5 scale.

A higher HPIA score indicates better overall hospital performance relative to national peers.

Reporting period

HPIA is reported annually. The reporting year shown reflects the most recent available dataset.

🔬

CliSQI Informational

Clinical Services Quality Indicators (CliSQI) are specific, measurable clinical outcome metrics tracked at the hospital level. They complement HPIA by providing granular clinical data.

Examples of CliSQI indicators include:

  • Surgical Site Infection Rate
  • Unplanned Readmission Rate
  • Hospital-Acquired Infection Rate
  • Mortality rates for specific conditions
  • Blood transfusion rates

CliSQI is displayed as an informational indicator. It does not currently contribute to the overall composite score under the 2025 methodology.

🏘️

Cluster Performance 25% weight

KKM organises hospitals into geographically linked clusters. Each cluster consists of at least one specialist or lead hospital and multiple supporting hospitals in the same area.

Cluster performance measures how effectively hospitals within a cluster collaborate, share resources, and coordinate patient care pathways.

Cluster roles

  • Lead Hospital — typically the main specialist hospital in the cluster, responsible for governance and coordination
  • Non-Lead Hospital — district or secondary hospitals that refer and receive patients from the lead hospital

A hospital's cluster score reflects both individual performance and contribution to cluster-wide outcomes.

💬

MyPSQ / PSQ-18 15% weight

MyPSQ (Malaysian Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire) is based on the PSQ-18 (Patient Satisfaction Questionnaire-18), an internationally validated instrument for measuring patient-reported experience.

The PSQ-18 covers:

  • General satisfaction
  • Technical quality (skills and competence of staff)
  • Interpersonal manner (communication and respect)
  • Communication (explanations and information)
  • Financial aspects (for applicable settings)
  • Time spent with doctors
  • Accessibility and convenience

Patient satisfaction reflects reported patient experience and should be considered alongside clinical and operational quality indicators. It does not directly measure clinical outcomes.

📋

Casemix 10% weight

Casemix classifies hospital patients into groups based on clinical and resource characteristics. It provides a measure of the complexity and resource intensity of cases handled.

Key Casemix concepts

  • Case Mix Index (CMI) — the average relative weight of all cases; a higher CMI indicates more complex cases
  • Diagnosis Related Groups (DRG) — groupings of clinically similar cases that consume similar resources
  • Severity of Illness — measures how severe patient conditions are on admission

Casemix performance measures how efficiently a hospital manages its case complexity. A higher Casemix score does not automatically mean better care — it reflects resource management relative to case complexity.

Data Quality & Missing Data

This platform uses the following data availability states to clearly communicate data quality:

StatusMeaning
AvailableData is available and valid for the reporting period
Not availableData should exist but is missing from the dataset
PendingData collection is in progress for this period
Not applicableThis indicator does not apply to this hospital type
SuppressedData exists but is withheld (e.g. small numbers rule)
EstimatedValue is an estimate, not a directly measured figure

Missing values are never displayed as zero. A hospital with no MyPSQ data shows "Not available", not "0%".

The source of each data point is the KKM dataset for the relevant reporting year. Calculated presentation metrics (such as derived overall scores) are clearly distinguished from official source figures.
